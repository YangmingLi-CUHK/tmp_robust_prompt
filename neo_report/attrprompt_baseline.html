2. Key Observations

  • GCN Teacher alone is highly robust — only 0.9pp drop from clean to M-0.25. The Metattack perturbation (up to 25% edges added) barely affects a 5-shot GCN, likely because global edge perturbation doesn't concentrate on the 35 labeled nodes' neighborhoods.
  • AttrPrompt x preserves teacher performance on clean data (68.81% vs 68.91%), but amplifies degradation under attack (−3.3pp vs teacher's −0.9pp). The adversarial PGD training on edges does not transfer to Metattack defense.
  • AttrPrompt dynamic (paper's default, IB=True, norm_if=True) collapses to ~38% across all ptb rates — a 30pp degradation from teacher. The KNN-graph gating mechanism + adversarial PGD causes severe distribution shift. This holds despite the norm_if bug being fixed.
